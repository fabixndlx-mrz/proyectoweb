# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML and CSS with native JavaScript; Anime.js 4.2.0 powers the rotating split-text phrases on the two page titles.

## Users

Students in the Software Development career who need to explore their curriculum and use a practical financial education project. Teachers and evaluators are a secondary audience for presenting and reviewing the student work.

## Product Purpose

Present the Software Development curriculum through an interactive module portfolio and demonstrate applied learning through Mi Finanzas, an educational personal budgeting and savings tool. Success means visitors can understand the learning path and use the financial calculations without assistance.

## Positioning

The project connects the Web and Mobile Applications module to a usable personal-finance learning tool, while showing how the broader curriculum contributes to its development.

## Operating Context

Visitors navigate between the curriculum portfolio and Mi Finanzas. In the finance tool, they enter monthly income, categorized expenses, a savings goal, and current savings, then review calculated totals and practical consumption guidance. Financial entries are intentionally not retained after the page is reloaded.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio includes the three requested generic modules and six specialization modules, with descriptions, professional utility, and key learning outcomes.
- Mi Finanzas calculates available funds, savings progress, and a consolidated financial summary from the visitor's current inputs.
- The initial implementation is a static, responsive web experience using semantic HTML, CSS, and native JavaScript. No backend or account system is requested.
- User-entered financial data exists only in the current page session and is cleared on reload.
- Visitors can switch between light and dark themes from either page; the theme preference is saved separately from financial entries.
- Curriculum descriptions should be presented as explanatory learning content, not quoted or independently verified official curriculum text.

## Brand Commitments

- The finance project name is "Mi Finanzas: Educación y Control Financiero".
- The interface and educational copy are in Spanish.
- The requested palette uses professional institutional blues and emerald green accents, with light neutral surfaces.
- The voice is professional, clear, and encouraging.

## Evidence on Hand

- The product brief supplies the general training objective and the names of nine required curriculum modules.
- No official curriculum document, institutional logo, approved photography, or verified financial data was supplied. Do not invent institutional endorsements, official-source claims, or user testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Make the curriculum easy to scan and understand.
- Connect learning outcomes to practical professional use.
- Make financial calculations transparent and responsive to input.
- Keep personal financial entries on the current page session only.
- Use clear Spanish labels and accessible interactions.
